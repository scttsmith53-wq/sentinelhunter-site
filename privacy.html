Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Sentinel Hunter is an Infinity Digital Development company. This policy explains what happens when you visit sentinelhunter.co, and what happens if we email your business from this domain. Infinity Digital Development is based in Denver, Colorado, and publishes its main site at infinitydigitaldev.com.

This website

sentinelhunter.co is a static website. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not include a form, a store, or advertising. We do not set analytics or advertising cookies here.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may keep ordinary technical logs, such as an IP address, browser type, and the page requested, in order to run and protect the site. We use that information only for those purposes.

Email we send

We send business email about AI websites, AI chat and automation, and lead capture. Those services are provided by Infinity Digital Development. A message from this domain is a business message to a business contact. It identifies Sentinel Hunter and Infinity Digital Development.

We send email to people we believe may want to hear about that work. We do not sell or rent contact information, and we do not buy consumer marketing lists for this domain.

Information we use

For outreach, we may use a name, role, company name, and business email address. We take those details from public business sources, or from you if you write or call us.

If you use the contact page on infinitydigitaldev.com, that page is operated by Infinity Digital Development and any details you submit there are handled by that company so it can reply.

How to opt out

Every outreach email includes a way to opt out. You can:

  • Use the unsubscribe link in the message, or
  • Reply to the email and ask us to stop, or
  • Call 720-252-7037, or
  • Reach us through the Infinity Digital Development contact page.

If you opt out, we stop emailing that address. You can also ask what business contact information we have for you and ask us to delete it. Use the phone number or the contact page, and say which address to remove.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. Email delivery and website hosting providers process contact details and technical logs only so they can send the mail or serve this site. We do not authorize them to use that information for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep outreach contact details until you opt out, until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them to send or record these business messages. Technical logs are kept for the period the host retains them for security and operations.

Children

This site and these emails are meant for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. The current version is the one published at sentinelhunter.co/privacy.html.
